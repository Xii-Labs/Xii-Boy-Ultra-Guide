# Materials and Tools

To make the **Xii-Boy Ultra** build as simple as possible, we’ve organized the required materials and tools into an easily accessible list. Amazon links are provided whenever possible, but note that cheaper prices may be avaiable at other retailers such as Aliexpress.

## Soldering Tools

- **Soldering Iron:** [Pinecil V2](https://a.co/d/gzenrbS)  
- **Soldering Iron (Alternative):** [Queco T12 956 soldering station](https://a.co/d/5TNCGkn)  
- **Solder:** [Kester SN60PB40 0.031"](https://a.co/d/bS10PMz)  
- **Flux:** [Stirri V2-TF](https://a.co/d/fC5cPg5)  
- **Flux (Alternative):** [Chip Quik SMD291](https://a.co/d/8gm59Zs)  
- **Solder Braid:** [MG Chemicals Fine Braid Super Wick](https://a.co/d/gPIm9nL)  
- **Hot Air Gun (Optional):** [Hot Air Gun](https://a.co/d/hCR4eZt)  
:::details Why is it Optional?
A **Hot Air Gun** or **Hot Air Rework Station** is useful for heating large components or many components at once. However, it often takes longer than a Soldering Iron to transfer heat, and it risks melting or blowing parts around. On the Wii motherboard, a Hot Air Gun is the most practical way to remove the `1.8V LDO`. Removal is also possible with a **Dremel** and **Soldering Iron** if done carefully.
:::

## Hand Tools

- **Dremel:** [Dremel Tool](https://a.co/d/eT733dP)  
- **Diamond Cutting Wheels:** [Diamond Cutting Wheels](https://a.co/d/ixgOG9f)  
:::details Will Other Types of Wheels Work?
**No!** The default wheels are not sharp enough and will struggle to cut through the Wii motherboard. This makes it more likely to **skip** and unintentionally cut components **or yourself**. Wheels with teeth (like on a table saw) are more rare on  dremels, but they are **extremely dangerous** and should be **avoided at all costs** as well.
:::
- **Pliers (Needle Nose and Diagonal Cutting):** [Pliers Set](https://a.co/d/cr09Rg4)  
- **Fine Tipped Tweezers:** [Tweezers](https://a.co/d/5OKSr2a)  
- **Screwdriver Set:** [Screwdriver Set](https://a.co/d/jk59Y1K)  
:::details Tri-Point (Y0) Screwdriver Length
Ensure the Tri-Point (Y0) screwdriver has a long shaft to reach recessed screws in the Wii. The linked set has one with the correct sizing.
:::

:::details What to Search For
Broken Wiis can often be found in the **$20-$25** range after shipping. When portabilizing, the disc drive is scrapped, so search for **4-layer Wiis** with non-functional drives. Use the **[Wii Revision Checker](https://wii-revision.netlify.app/)** and refer to [Wii Preparation](https://xbu-guide.me/wii-preparation#wii-preparation) to confirm the Wii is 4-layers before purchasing.
:::
